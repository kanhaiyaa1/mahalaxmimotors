Service Technician

Full Time Multiple Openings All Levels Welcome

Perform vehicle maintenance, diagnostics, and repairs using advanced automotive technology and genuine Maruti parts.

Requirements: ITI/Diploma in Automobile
Experience: Freshers to 5+ years
Certification: Bronze/Silver/Advanced
Location: Shahabad Markanda

Service Advisor

Full Time Customer Facing

Act as the point of contact between customers and service department. Provide technical advice, estimate costs, and ensure customer satisfaction.

Requirements: Graduate with technical knowledge
Experience: 2-5 years preferred
Skills: Communication & Technical expertise
Language: Hindi, English (Punjabi plus)

Customer Relationship Manager

Full Time High Growth Potential

Manage customer relationships, handle insurance claims, coordinate warranty services, and ensure exceptional customer experience throughout the service journey.

Skills: Insurance knowledge preferred
Personality: Excellent communication

Parts & Inventory Manager

Skills: Computer proficiency, DMS knowledge
Mindset: Detail-oriented & organized
